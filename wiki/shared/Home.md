### What is FOXSkullAPI?
* **Easy-to-use** - FOXSkullAPI is easy-to-use with chainable methods allowing you to manage your skulls
* **Version agnostic** - FOXSkullAPI and all its methods will work on any Minecraft version Figura supports
* **Capable** - Everything you can do in any other skull system should be possible, and easier, with FOXSkullAPI
* **Feature rich** - FOXSkullAPI comes with a variety of features like custom item models, NBT/Component readers, etc
